- **A refinement rewrote the idea, not its hyperparameters.** Four of the five components were replaced and one removed outright, and the method kept its name [p. 40] (image) [p. 47]; §3.2 describes engineering as "hyperparameter tuning or code adjustments" [§3.2] (tex:sections/3_new_method.tex:45) (A-ART-5, U-ART-11).

- **The final paper is a 16-page ICLR 2025 anonymous submission with 12 tables, 5 figures, 7 ablations and 42 references, and no variance anywhere** [pp. 56–71]. Rebuttal additions are not marked, and it contradicts itself in four places (A-ART-7 to A-ART-10) [pp. 56–71].
